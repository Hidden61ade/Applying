## Context

A meta-narrative experiment exploring how the *frame* of a game — windows, prompts, file systems — can become its narrator. The script (16,000 words) branches through themes of isolation, presence, and the small rebellions of agency.

## My Role

- Authored the entire branching script and dialogue tree.
- Built specialized C# logic for simulated OS interactions and meta-level feedback.
- Designed the merging of literary narrative with systemic mechanics.

## Why It Matters

This is the project where I stopped asking *what story does the game tell* and started asking *what story does the interface tell*. The answer changed how I design.
